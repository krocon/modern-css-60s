Road trip · 9 days

Nine days along the northern coast

Scroll down: the header should stay on top the whole way.

Day 1

Leaving the city

We packed the van at dawn and followed the river north. By noon the traffic was gone and the road turned into a thin grey line between fields.

Day 2

The first harbour

Fishing boats, a bakery that opens at five, and a harbour master who knows every boat by its sound. We stayed two nights instead of one.

Day 3

Wind and lighthouses

Three lighthouses in one day. The last one lets you climb all the way up, if you can handle the wind and a hundred and twelve steps.

Day 4

Rain day

Rain all day, so we read, cooked soup on the camping stove and planned the rest of the route on a paper map like it was 1995.

Day 5

The long beach

Eleven kilometres of sand and almost nobody on it. We walked until the tide turned and then raced it back to the dunes.

Day 6

Island hopping

Two ferries, one island without cars and a café that only sells waffles. Bikes are the only way to get around, and that is the point.

Day 7

Back inland

Forests replace the coast. The air smells of pine and the evenings get quiet. We found a lake with a wooden jetty and stayed until dark.

Day 8

Home again

Nine days, one flat tyre, zero regrets. The van needs a wash, the map needs new folds, and we already know where we go next year.
